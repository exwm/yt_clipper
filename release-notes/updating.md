## Installing and Updating

* Install the `markup script` from [Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/543460-yt-clipper) with a user script extension such as Tampermonkey ([install guide](https://greasyfork.org/en/help/installing-user-scripts)). The extension keeps it up to date.
* Download the `clipper script` for your platform below and extract it into the folder that holds your yt_clipper installs. Keep that folder out of your downloads folder. See [this readme section](https://github.com/exwm/yt_clipper#updating) to carry your settings over from an earlier version.
