## Updating

* The `markup script` updates itself when installed from [Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/543460-yt-clipper).
* For the `clipper script`, download the zip for your platform below and extract it next to your current install. See [this readme section](https://github.com/exwm/yt_clipper#updating) to keep your settings across versions.
